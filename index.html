Hierarchical Model with Two Lights: Desk Lamp

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the model.
Use the arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera.
Use the slider and dropdown to rotate about an axis; the button starts/stops spinning.
The lamp's spotlight is carried by the arm, so moving any joint aims the light.

Primitives: cube (desk, upper arm), cylinder (lamp base, lower arm), cone (shade), sphere (bulb, two balls), torus (shade rim).
Levels: desk (root) → lamp base → lower arm → upper arm → shade → bulb and rim. The two balls sit on the desk.
Lights: room light (fixed in the world) and the lamp's spotlight.

Outstanding Effects Attempt:

  • The spotlight moves with the lamp.
  • The bulb glows when the spotlight is on.
  • The spotlight fades with distance.
  • Spotlight color, brightness and width controls.
  • 5 primitives and 6 levels.
  • Stretched shapes still light correctly.
  • Reset button.
